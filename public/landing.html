How It Works

From ancient wisdom to modern insights in just four simple steps.

1

Write, Speak, or Create

Open your handcrafted journal and write with ink, record a voice entry, or make visual art. Choose whichever suits the day.

2

Tap to Connect

Tap the journal's NFC chip to your phone. No app to find, no password — the day's entry opens itself.

3

Claude Reads It Back

Claude transcribes the page, the audio, or reads the colour and composition — then tells you what it actually sees, including the parts you skirted.

4

Watch the Pattern Emerge

One entry is a mood. Six is a pattern. Καιρός tracks what keeps returning — and says so plainly when nothing has shifted.

Built in Austria

From Coffee Shop to AI Innovation

A teaching student who had never written code, working part-time at a village coffee shop, building a journaling app with Claude AI as the development partner.

Made in Austria

"Everyone should be able to write their own book"

At 30, I took a leave from university (High School Teachers' Education—Informatics & English) to pursue a dream. Working part-time at a local coffee shop in a small Austrian village to fund development, I read Marcus Aurelius' Meditations and watched my mother journal every day.

I thought: what if everyone could write their own Meditations? What if AI could help us understand our patterns like Marcus understood his?

"I had zero traditional coding skills. Just a clear vision and Claude AI as my development partner. Together, we built the entire app—50 journey paths, voice recording, NFC integration, everything. This is proof that you don't need to code to build technology. You need to understand people."

From chemistry student in Georgia → car accident → moving to Austria at 26 → 2 years learning German → starting a new degree → taking the leap to build Καιρός. Every struggle taught me something: resilience, patience, vision over perfection.

Built in the open

No testimonials yet. Here's the work instead.

Καιρός is early. Rather than borrow credibility it hasn't earned, here is precisely what has been built — all of it verifiable.

1,047

Prompts, written one at a time

Not templates with the nouns swapped. All 53 paths are hand-authored day by day — 50 included with every journal, plus three in the Moments pack — running from four-day sprints to hundred-day odysseys, each prompt written for its specific day and theme.

3

Languages, all the way down

English, German, and Georgian — and not just the buttons. All 1,047 journey prompts are translated, and the AI writes its analysis back to you in your language. Georgian gets its own typeface so the script renders properly rather than falling back.

3

Ways to make an entry

Photograph your handwriting, speak it aloud, or paint and sketch it. Claude reads all three — transcribing the page, transcribing the audio, or interpreting colour and composition when there are no words at all. Multi-modal paths ask for more than one in a single journey.

Your API key never touches the browser — every Claude call is proxied server-side. Entries are never used to train models.
